      Four properties that sound similar but control different things. Here's the simple breakdown.

  <section class="space-y-6 max-w-3xl mx-auto">
    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">The two axes</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      Flexbox has a <strong>main axis</strong> and a <strong>cross axis</strong>. In a row (<code class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded">flex-direction: row</code>), the main axis is horizontal and the cross axis is vertical. In a column, they flip.
    </p>

    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">*-items vs *-content</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      <strong><code class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded">*-items</code></strong> aligns <strong>each item</strong> within its cell or line. <code>justify-items</code> = main axis (left/right); <code>align-items</code> = cross axis (top/bottom). It affects the items themselves.
    </p>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      <strong><code class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded">*-content</code></strong> distributes <strong>extra space</strong>—either between items along the main axis, or between lines along the cross axis. It affects how leftover space is used, not the items' size.
    </p>

    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">justify-content</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      A <strong>*-content</strong> property. Distributes extra space along the <strong>main axis</strong> (horizontal in a row). Use it when the container is wider than the items and you want to space them out: <code class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded">flex-start</code>, <code>center</code>, <code>space-between</code>, etc.
    </p>

    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">justify-items</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      An <strong>*-items</strong> property. Aligns <strong>each item</strong> along the main axis within its cell. In Grid, this positions items left/center/right inside their grid cell. 
      <em>Note: Flexbox doesn't have <code>justify-items</code>—it's a Grid property.</em>
    </p>

    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">align-items</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      An <strong>*-items</strong> property. Aligns <strong>each item</strong> along the cross axis within its line. In a row, that's vertical (top, center, bottom). Use it when items have different heights and you want them aligned: <code class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded">stretch</code> (the flex/grid alignment keyword, not the separate property <a href="/l/width-stretch" class="inline whitespace-nowrap text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 underline font-mono text-sm px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50">width: stretch</a>), <code>center</code>, <code>flex-start</code>, <code>flex-end</code>.
    </p>

    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">align-content</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 leading-relaxed">
      A <strong>*-content</strong> property. Distributes extra space <strong>between lines</strong> along the cross axis. Only applies when you have <strong>multiple lines</strong> (<code class="text-purple-600 dark:text-purple-300 bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-900/50 px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded">flex-wrap: wrap</code>). If everything fits on one line, <code>align-content</code> does nothing.
    </p>
  </section>
